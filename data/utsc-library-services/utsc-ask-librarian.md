# Ask a Librarian chat

Get research help from anywhere! Select the "Chat with us" button to start a chat session. We can help you:

- Find sources about a topic
- Locate a specific book or article
- Use library services, resources, and tools effectively

## Hours

January 5th - April 11th, 2026

- Monday - Thursday: 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM
- Friday: 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- Saturday - Sunday: 12:00 PM - 6:00 PM

Closures: Monday, February 16th (Family Day); Friday, April 3rd (Good Friday).

## Privacy

Ask a Librarian chat collects only the minimum amount of information from visitors necessary to provide effective service. At the end of your chat, you can print or email a transcript of your session.
