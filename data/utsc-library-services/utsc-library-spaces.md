# Library Spaces

## Archives and Special Collections

Learn more about the Rare & unique materials housed in the Archive that support teaching and research at the U of T Scarborough.

## Course Reserves and Short Term Loan

Learn more about special materials that are placed on hold to support you in completing assigned course readings and other classroom work.

## Exhibits in our Vitrines

The library partners with members of our community to create physical exhibitions in two vitrines at the front of the library.

## Information and Reference Desk (Info Desk)

The information and reference desk is located at the front of the library and is staffed during regular hours of operation.

## Makerspace

A safe, welcoming space for participants to experiment and be knowledge creators. Learn more about our Makerspace!

## Mindfulness Space

Visit our newly moved mindfulness space with equipment and spaces to support well-being.

## One Button Studio

The One Button Studio is now open!

## Podcast Room

Record your podcast in AC 252A. The space features an audio mixer, microphones and headphones to help you create your own podcast, voice-over project or even live-streaming event.

## Renovations at the U of T Scarborough Library

The Library is undergoing a large renovation project (Last updated November 12, 2025)

## Reuse Depot

The Reuse Depot is a place for students and other library users to leave and/or take school and stationery supplies, such as notebooks, pens, calculators, etc.

## Seed Library

In the library, across from the main floor washrooms, you can 'check out' seeds at no charge. Take what you need and leave the rest for the others, save seeds from when you grow, and donate them back to keep the cycle going!

## Silent Study Space

The library has dedicated space available for silent study, in AC338. Learn more about where this space is located and policies governing the use of this space.

## Study Rooms

The library has eight small study rooms, which can be booked via the Intranet. Learn more about the number of study rooms and the policies that govern them.

## The BRIDGE

Where business, research and innovation converge, The BRIDGE is a unique partnership between the Library and the Department of Management. Learn more about this state-of-the-art facility.
